there are few things i love more than the general overarching field of dravidian linguistics. firstly, it is the rich source for my own mother tongue; in its nutritious soil Kannada (especially halegannada) and numerous others (to mention the T's: Tamil, Toda, my lingui pater: Tulu) has taken root, and blossomed. There is obvious affection for it, an affection that is borne out of familiarity and respect for the culture that, through some or the other way, gave rise to me. One should not be negligent of that- be what it may!
